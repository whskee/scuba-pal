# Safety Statement

This software is for simulation and dive logging MVP work only.

It must not be used for real dive safety decisions.

It is not a certified dive computer and does not calculate:

- decompression obligations
- no-decompression limits
- tissue loading
- decompression ceilings
- CNS oxygen exposure
- gas consumption
- gas mix planning
- emergency ascent guidance
- life-support recommendations

The ascent-rate status and safety-stop countdown in this MVP are simple display and logging features. They are not a complete dive safety system.

Real diving requires a certified dive computer, proper dive planning, suitable redundant equipment, and training from qualified professionals.

Any future hardware prototype must go through engineering validation, environmental testing, formal risk analysis, and applicable certification work before being considered for real dive use.
